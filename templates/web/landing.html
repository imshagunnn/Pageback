How it works

1

Mark your place

Tell PageBack which chapter you have reached. That chapter becomes the boundary. Nothing after it is used.

2

Catch up in your time

A 30-second jolt, a two-minute walk back in, or a five-minute sitting with the story — you choose the depth.

3

Ask, then continue

Who is this? What changed? What should I remember? Answers come only from pages you have already read.
